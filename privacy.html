Privacy notice

Your privacy

A plain explanation of what personal information this website collects and what I do with it. Last updated 27 November 2026.

Who I am

This website is run by Lily Zham, trading as Lilyz Design Studio, a freelance brand designer based in London, United Kingdom. I am responsible for the personal information described on this page. If you have any question about it, use the Email me link at the bottom of this page.

What I collect

When you send an inquiry through the form on this site, I receive the answers you give: your name or business name, your email address, where you are based, a link to your website or social media, what you tell me about your business and project, your estimated budget and timeline, and how you found me.

If you email me directly, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

I do not ask for payment details, identity documents or any sensitive personal information through this website.

How I use it

I use your details only to reply to your inquiry, to prepare a proposal, and, if we work together, to carry out and manage your project. I do not sell your information, and I do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask me to.

My legal basis

Under UK data protection law I rely on two grounds: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and my legitimate interest in answering people who contact me about my services.

Who helps me process it

Inquiry form answers are collected and stored with Google Forms, and my email is provided by Google. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps technical logs, including visitors' IP addresses, for security. These companies may store data on servers outside the United Kingdom and apply their own safeguards and privacy policies.

I do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics and does not carry advertising trackers. Links to Instagram, Behance, LinkedIn and Google open those companies' own sites, which have their own privacy policies.

How long I keep it

I keep inquiry details for as long as I need them to respond to you. If we work together, I keep project correspondence for the length of the project and afterwards as part of my business records. You can ask me to delete your details at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the information I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict how I use it. To do any of these, email me and I will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
