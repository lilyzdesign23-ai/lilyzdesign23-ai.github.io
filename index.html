How it works

Four calm steps

A clear process, so you always know where we are and what comes next.

01

Discover

We talk about your business, your customers and what you are dreaming of. I ask a lot of questions so the design has something real to stand on.

02

Direction

I gather the thinking into a mood board: colours, type, textures and references. We agree the direction together before any logos are drawn.

03

Design

The identity takes shape. I present the logo suite, palette and typography, shown on real items so you can see the brand living in the world.

04

Deliver

You receive organised, ready-to-use files: logos in every format, colour codes, patterns, print-ready artwork and social media assets.

FAQ

Good questions

Something else on your mind? Just ask

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mostly independent businesses that care about the details: bakeries, florists, fashion labels, food and drink brands and other makers. If you are starting something new or feel you have outgrown your current look, we will probably get on well.

What will I receive at the end of a project?

A complete, organised set of brand files. That usually means your logo suite in different formats for print and screen, your colour codes, typography, patterns, and any packaging, print or social media designs we agreed on.

How does a project start?

Send me a message with a few lines about your business and what you need. We will have a chat to see if we are a good fit, and I will follow up with a proposal that sets out the scope, timeline and price before anything begins.

How long does it take?

The Brand Starter Pack takes around 2 weeks. The Premium Brand Identity Suite and the Social Media Booster take 4 to 6 weeks, depending on refinements and feedback. Packaging timelines are agreed project by project.

How much does it cost?

Packages start from $500, and each one is listed with its price range in the services section. Payment plans are available, and packaging projects are quoted individually. Any package can be adjusted to your needs and budget in our discovery call.

I already have a logo. Can you refresh my brand?

Yes. A rebrand can keep what your customers already love and refine everything around it. The Sweet Cake Co. project is a good example of this.
