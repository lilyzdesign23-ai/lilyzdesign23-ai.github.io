04 / 05 · Floristry

Daily Daisy

Daily Daisy is a florist built around a simple idea: happiness looks like fresh flowers. The identity follows a bouquet all the way from the shop window to the doorstep.

Industry
Floristry
Scope
Logo suite · Packaging · Print · Signage
The challenge

Flowers are the star, so the brand has to frame them without competing. It also needed to stay recognisable across very different surfaces, from a ribbon a few centimetres wide to a flower box and a shopfront.

The approach

A soft aqua carries the packaging, with plum as the accent and cream for print. An oval emblem, a four-petal mark and a DD monogram can each stand on their own.

The details do the talking: a letterbox box that fits through the door, ribbon printed with the mark, and a thank-you card that reads “the reason for your daily smile is here”.
