Steep & Stir

Steep & Stir reimagines the art of the British afternoon tea with a refined, mature design made for discerning drinkers. Loose leaf tea isn't just a brew. It is a sensory ritual where full, unbroken leaves unfurl to release a depth of aroma and flavour that tea bags can't capture.

Industry
Loose leaf tea
Scope
Logo suite · Packaging · Print · Social media
The challenge

Busy adults are caught in fast-paced schedules, with little time to pause, disconnect or properly decompress during the day.

Traditional loose leaf tea can feel intimidating, messy or fussy for someone short on time, so they default to quick tea bags that strip away the ritual.

The approach

The brand bridges luxury and daily life by turning the act of steeping loose leaf tea in a teapot into an accessible, grounding routine.

A playful yet mature identity leans on rich aromas and intense flavours: an ampersand mark shaped like a teapot, copper-lidded tins colour-coded by blend, and deep green packaging that feels like a gift.
