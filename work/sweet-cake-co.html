Sweet Cake Co.

Sweet Cake Co. is a confectionery artist with a loyal following and a look that had not kept up with the care in the baking. The rebrand keeps the name and the taste, and gives everything around them a more delicate, refined and intentional feel.

Industry
Confectionery
Scope
Rebrand · Logo suite · Packaging · Social media
The challenge

The brand needed to feel as considered as the cakes themselves, and to hold together across many small touchpoints: boxes, labels, bags, menus, uniforms and a busy Instagram page.

The approach

An ornate framed logo is paired with a flowing SC monogram that works alone as a seal, sticker or stamp. A lattice pattern built from the monogram lines boxes, tissue and totes.

The launch was planned as part of the identity: highlight icons, story templates and a short series of announcement slides to introduce the new look to existing customers.
