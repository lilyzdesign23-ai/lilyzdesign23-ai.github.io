03 / 05 · Cosmetic surgery clinic

Dr. Hamed Ghahari

An identity for the cosmetic surgery clinic of Dr. Hamed Ghahari. The brand works in two languages, English and Persian, and carries the same quiet confidence from the reception wall to the card a patient takes home.

Industry
Cosmetic surgery clinic
Scope
Logo · Stationery · Uniforms · Signage
The challenge

A clinic has to earn trust before anyone walks through the door. The identity needed to feel professional and reassuring, and to read just as clearly in Persian as it does in English.

The approach

The emblem is an interlaced geometric knot, set beside the doctor's name in either script. Deep teal with a warm sand accent keeps the tone clinical but never cold.

The mark is applied wherever patients and staff meet the clinic: reception signage, scrubs, letterhead and envelopes, business cards with a QR code, mugs and the website.
