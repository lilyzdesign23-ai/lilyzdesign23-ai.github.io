The Cookie Edit

The Cookie Edit is a cookie line by Sweet Cake Co. It shares the family burgundy, and adds a wink: candy stripes, a fashion-illustration character and the line “Sweetness in style”.

Industry
Cookies, by Sweet Cake Co.
Scope
Logo · Illustration · Packaging
The challenge

The new line had to feel related to Sweet Cake Co. while having a personality of its own, lighter and more playful than the main brand.

The approach

The logo stacks expressive lettering beside an illustrated figure in a beret, coffee in hand. Pink and cream stripes run across boxes and paper bags, and the mark moves easily onto totes and keyrings.
